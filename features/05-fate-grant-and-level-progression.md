# Feature: Fate Grant and Level Progression

## Capability Description

Provides a card-based draft acquisition mechanic upon character creation and each subsequent level milestone, replacing static starting loadouts with dynamic build progression.

## Key Capabilities & Authoritative Seed Rules

- **Zero Starting Inventory Baseline:**
  - New characters begin the game with completely empty action slots, backpack inventory, and equipment paperdoll slots.
- **Initial Game-Start Fate Grant (Level 1):**
  - Upon selecting a vocation and entering the dungeon at Level 1, an automated Fate Grant draft sequence immediately triggers.
  - The player is presented with a random draw of 5 offered cards/items weighted by class suitability and initial luck/rarity.
  - The player selects 1 to 2 items/spells from the draw to form their initial foundational build and starting action loadout.
- **Milestone XP & Level-Up Fate Grants:**
  - Defeating dungeon monsters and clearing exploration milestones awards Experience Points (XP).
  - Upon reaching EVERY level-up milestone, an automated Fate Grant triggers.
  - The player receives a new random draw of 5 cards/items (featuring enhanced rarities, powerful spells, advanced weapons, or passive relics) and selects 1 to 2 options to continuously expand their build.

*(Note: Card pool weighting algorithms, rarity tiers, XP leveling curves, and draft selection confirmation flows are owned by Stage 3 briefs.)*
