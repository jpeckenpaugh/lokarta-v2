# Feature: Playable Vocations and Combat Archetypes

## Capability Description

Provides four distinct playable character classes with specialized attribute profiles, offensive and defensive combat mechanics, and thematic ability kits.

## Key Capabilities & Authoritative Seed Rules

- **Four Playable Vocations:**
  - **Magician:**
    - Profile: Fragile health pool, high mana pool.
    - Kit & Identity: Offensive arcane and elemental magic, ranged wand attacks (e.g. Wand Spark), Light illumination aura spells, and piercing line strikes (e.g. Energy Beam penetrating enemies in a 4-tile linear path).
  - **Archer:**
    - Profile: Balanced health and mana pools.
    - Kit & Identity: Ranged physical marksmanship, standard bow shots requiring line-of-sight and ammunition (Arrows) or focus, and heavy single-target physical burst abilities (e.g. Power Shot).
  - **Fighter:**
    - Profile: High health pool, high physical resilience.
    - Kit & Identity: Melee physical mastery, sweeping sword strikes, multi-target cleaves, and sturdy close-quarters defense.
  - **Paladin:**
    - Profile: Durable hybrid health and mana pool.
    - Kit & Identity: Holy magic, healing prayers, protective defensive auras/shields, and heavy crushing melee warhammer strikes.
